Your daily SID routine

Three steps. That's the whole day.

  1. 1
    Open Indeed and refresh the page each morning before you start (press F5). This keeps SID working smoothly.
  2. 2
    Click Start on each job to download the candidates. Do all your jobs for the day.
  3. 3
    When ALL your jobs are done, click “Send all to Melissa.” That's it — you're finished. You'll see “✓ Sent — Melissa has all of them.”

Good to know

  • You don't need to download or upload anything. “Send all to Melissa” sends it directly. The Download buttons at the bottom are only a backup, for if Melissa asks.
  • Send once at the end is best — but if you tap Send more than once, or send job-by-job, that's totally fine. It all gets sorted into one on Melissa's side.
  • The stats still show what was processed and skipped — nothing there changed.

If something looks off

It says “⚠ didn't send — tap Send again.”
Your internet probably hiccuped. Just tap Send all to Melissa again. Nothing was lost.
You closed the browser before it finished.
No problem — open Indeed again and SID quietly finishes sending on its own. Keep the tab open a moment.
It says “Indeed may have changed — tell Melissa.”
That means Indeed updated something. Don't worry — just tell Melissa and she'll sort it out quickly.
You need an old day's files.
Open Job History in SID — you can re-download past days from there.
